## 2. 01:50–04:50 What Grok Bot is
- Bots tied to specific jobs (e.g. chief of staff, sales outbound, inbox manager); you message them like teammates.
- Bots remember: memory/context improves with use.
- Settings: bots log in to tools like you do; routines (automations) tracked per bot; shareable templates on the marketplace.
- Own computer: sign in to Luma once, it remembers. Finishes the work. Always on in the cloud, runs 24/7 after you shut the laptop. Feels like iMessage, on phone and laptop.
